Six things I've built, from a bin to a study bot

Some work, some half-work. All of them taught me something the lecture slides didn't.

  1. 01

    Smart Waste Bin

    A bin that opens its own lid when you walk up to it and reports how full it is to a live web dashboard over Wi-Fi.

    We built it as a team and presented it in the lab.

    Parts list

    • MCUESP32
    • SensingUltrasonic
    • LidServo motor
    • OutputWeb dashboard
    • Built byTeam

    What you're seeing

  2. 02

    OCR Reading Wand

    A handheld device for visually impaired people. Point it at printed text and it reads the words aloud through bone conduction earphones, so your ears stay free to hear what's around you.

    The target cost is GHS 350 to 500. If it isn't affordable, it doesn't help anyone.

    Parts list

    • BoardRaspberry Pi Zero 2W
    • TextTesseract OCR
    • VoiceeSpeak-ng
    • AudioBone conduction
    • CourseCPEN 104
  3. 03

    Telegram AI Bot

    A bot with trivia, task reminders and AI chat that remembers each user.

    The part I'm proudest of is /study. It pulls the text out of my lecture slides, finds the chunks closest to a question, and has Gemini answer from those chunks instead of guessing.

    Stack

    • LanguagePython
    • Botpython-telegram-bot
    • ModelGemini 2.5 Flash
    • PDFsPyMuPDF
    • Searchsentence-transformers
    • MathsNumPy
  4. 04

    RFID Access Control

    Tap a card and the door decides whether you get in. An Arduino reads the card's ID through an MFRC522 reader and checks it against the list of allowed cards.

    Parts list

    • MCUArduino
    • ReaderMFRC522
    • InputRFID cards
  5. 05

    Bongo Water Filter Analysis

    Is this water filter design worth building? A cost and risk study with dashboards for total cost of ownership (TCO), net present value (NPV) and failure modes and effects analysis (FMEA).

    Tools

    • ModellingMATLAB
    • DashboardsExcel
    • MethodsTCO, NPV, FMEA
